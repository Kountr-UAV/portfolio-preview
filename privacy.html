Who we are

Kountr Limited is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 02994226. Our registered office is 6 Ridgeway, Epsom, Surrey, KT19 8LB.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email services@kountr.co.uk or write to our registered office.

Information we use and where it comes from

When you contact us, we use the name, contact details, business or website details, project information and correspondence you provide. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that we do not need for your enquiry.

For business research, we use public company and professional information from company websites and Companies House. This can include a professional name or role, published business contact details, a company website and notes about a relevant website improvement. We use Hunter to check selected business email addresses.

Cloudflare receives technical connection information, such as your IP address and browser request information, to deliver and secure this website. The website details below explain the enquiry form and demonstration features.

Why we use information

Responding to enquiries and managing business relationships
We use your information to answer questions, discuss a project and maintain correspondence. Our legitimate interests are providing website services and managing relevant business relationships. Where you personally ask us to take steps towards a contract, or are personally party to a contract, we rely on those contract steps or the contract itself as appropriate.
Researching and contacting relevant businesses
Our legitimate interest is identifying businesses with a relevant website-services need and making proportionate professional contact. We consider relevance, reasonable expectations and your rights. Where electronic marketing rules require consent, consent is required separately; legitimate interests do not replace it.
Optional marketing you choose
Where we ask you to opt in to occasional marketing, we rely on your consent. That choice is separate from making an enquiry, and you can withdraw it at any time.
Security, records and legal responsibilities
Our legitimate interests include keeping the website secure and maintaining appropriate business records. We also keep or use information where necessary to meet applicable legal obligations, including accounting obligations if work goes ahead.

You do not have to make an enquiry or agree to marketing. We need enough contact and project information to respond; without it, we may be unable to help. A marketing choice does not affect our response to your enquiry.

You can stop direct marketing at any time

You have an absolute right to object to the use of your personal information for direct marketing. Reply to our email with “no thanks”, or email services@kountr.co.uk. You do not need to give a reason.

You can use the same routes to withdraw marketing consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew it. We keep only the minimal contact details and objection record needed to recognise your preference and avoid contacting you again for marketing.

Service providers

  • Google Workspace supports our email and business correspondence.
  • Cloudflare hosts and secures this website. Its Turnstile service provides a security check when the secure enquiry form is available.
  • Hunter checks selected business email addresses used in prospect research.
  • OpenAI supports AI-assisted business research and drafting. Research material and drafting prompts may include relevant public professional information or business correspondence.

These providers and their subprocessors may process personal information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Their applicable data processing terms provide safeguards recognised in the UK, including adequacy arrangements where applicable and standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum where required.

For information about the safeguards that apply to your information, or how to obtain a copy, email services@kountr.co.uk.

Website, cookies and demonstration features

This website’s own scripts do not set cookies, run advertising pixels or use analytics trackers. Fonts, images and demonstration pages are served from our website. Cloudflare handles technical connection information for hosting and security.

The FOLD shop demonstration saves sample basket product identifiers, pack choices and quantities in your browser’s local storage under fold-demo-basket-v1. It does not place an order or send basket contents to us. You can clear this data using your browser’s site-data controls. The other demonstration forms prepare local summaries; they do not send an enquiry or make a booking.

The website contact section prepares a local call brief; it cannot send a message or book a call. Please email us to make an enquiry. If a secure enquiry form is made available, it will request your name or business, reply email and message, with an optional website and separate marketing choice. Cloudflare’s Turnstile check receives technical browser and connection information; the form verifies the check before accepting a submission. The connection IP used for that verification is not forwarded in the enquiry payload.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and correspondence records while needed to respond, manage ongoing or agreed work, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. The period depends on the nature and progress of the enquiry, the relationship and any legal record-keeping requirements.

We review prospect information for continuing relevance, accuracy and contact preferences, and remove information that is no longer needed. A minimal suppression record may be retained for as long as needed to honour a marketing objection; we do not retain the full prospect history solely for that purpose.

Your rights and complaints

You can ask to access your personal information, correct inaccuracies, erase information or restrict its use where the legal conditions apply. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also have a right to receive and reuse information where processing is automated and based on consent or a contract. Some rights depend on why we use the information and the circumstances.

Contact services@kountr.co.uk to make a request. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to contact us before doing so.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our use of personal information changes. The date above identifies the current version. Where a change requires us to tell you directly, we will do so.

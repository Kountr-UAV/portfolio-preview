GOOD TO KNOW

A couple of
common questions.

Is my job too small?+

The imagined Goodfolk service includes small domestic jobs: a dripping tap, a loose fitting or a toilet that keeps running. Choose “Leaks & little fixes” to try the demo brief.

Can I get a price through this site?+

This fictional concept doesn’t quote or take payments. A real local tradesperson would discuss the details and scope before agreeing a price.

Do you do gas or emergency call-outs?+

This concept covers planned domestic plumbing and minor home repairs. It does not offer gas work or emergency response, and represents no certifications or accreditations.

What happens to my demo brief?+

It stays in this page while you use it. You can preview it and choose to download a text copy. Nothing is sent, booked or stored by the website.

Our point of view

Less noise.
More feeling.

A bowl should feel good in your hands. A mug should make you pause. FOLD imagines homewares with soft edges, grounded colours and room for the small imperfections that make an object interesting.

FOLD is a fictional brand created for Kountr’s portfolio. These objects, their materials and prices are an illustrative design concept, not real products offered for sale.

Good to know

A few small details.

A considered store makes the practical things easy to find, too.

Materials & finishes

The concept collection explores speckled stoneware, satin glazes and unglazed feet. All product images are AI-generated illustrations. Colour and texture are design references; no real material or food-safety claims are made.

Caring for ceramics

For real ceramics, follow the maker’s care instructions. Hand washing with a soft cloth is often a gentle option. The products here are fictional, so dishwasher, microwave and food-safety suitability have not been tested.

About this demo store

You can filter objects, choose single or pair packs and try the basket. It saves locally in your browser when storage is available. There is no checkout, payment, delivery or order submission.
